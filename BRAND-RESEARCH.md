## 3. What the place actually looks like (from 4 official photos)

Saved in `assets/photos/reference/`.

1. **Hammam / wet area** — white salt-brick vaulted ceiling, fiber-optic "starfield" ceiling, blue and magenta light, backlit Moorish arch with an ocean image, glass partitions, warm onyx light boxes. Reads as **underwater / cinematic**.
2. **Sauna** — dark wood, warm amber cove lighting, red glow on the floor. Warm, minimalist, cabin-like.
3. **Reception / lounge** — curved **blush-pink velvet** sofa, huge artificial **cherry-blossom tree**, faceted mirror ceiling panels, black-and-white striped marble floor, gold-veined stone, sheer curtains.
4. **Hammam 2** — full **ice-blue** salt-brick barrel vault, glowing niches, marble floor.

**Design language:** ocean/midnight blue + rose pink + warm brass + white salt stone. Maximalist, glossy, immersive, slightly surreal. Zellige geometry, arches, starry ceilings, water reflections.

> A guest review summed it up: *"as if you were somewhere underneath the ocean."* That is the brand.

## 4. The strategic conclusion

The reference site (`luxury-spa.framer.website`) is **warm beige minimalism** — sand, terracotta, matte, quiet.

Sabon Spa is the opposite: **deep ocean blue, pearl, blush pink, brass, glow, mirrors, stars**. Cloning the Framer template would actually dilute the brand.

**Recommended direction:** "Underwater luxury at night."
- Base: midnight navy `#07141F` → deep teal `#0C2A33`
- Pearl / bone: `#F4EFE8`
- Rose quartz (from the sofa): `#E9B7BE`
- Brass / onyx glow: `#C9A16B`
- Accent light: bioluminescent cyan `#6FD8D2`
- Type: a high-contrast serif (Playfair Display / Cormorant / Editorial New) + a clean grotesk (Geist / Inter)
- Motifs: arches, star ceilings (particle field), caustic light ripples, salt-brick texture, zellige linework.

### Where 3D fits perfectly
A WebGL **caustic light / underwater ripple** layer behind the hero, or a **slow starfield** echoing the hammam ceiling. Subtle, low-cost, on-brand — exactly the kind of React Three Fiber component we discussed. Not a gimmick; it *is* the interior.
